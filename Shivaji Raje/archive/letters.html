WISDOM OF THE KING महाराजांची शिकवण

Profound instructions, letters, and recorded statements that highlight his focus on justice, military discipline, and the welfare of his subjects. महाराजांची पत्रे आणि आज्ञापत्रे त्यांच्या न्यायबुद्धीची, लष्करी शिस्तीची आणि रयतेविषयीच्या काळजीची साक्ष देतात.

"
रयतेच्या भाजीच्या देठालाही हात लावू नका.
"Do not even touch a stem of vegetable belonging to the subjects without paying for it."
— Royal Edict to the Army
"
हिंदवी स्वराज्य व्हावे, हीच श्रींची इच्छा.
"It is the Almighty's wish that this Hindavi Swarajya should be established."
— Letter to Dadaji Naras Prabhu
"
स्त्रियांना माता समान मानणे, त्यांच्या सुरक्षिततेची जबाबदारी आपली आहे.
"Women should be treated as mothers; their protection is our foremost duty."
— Code of Conduct
"
किल्ले म्हणजे राज्याचे प्राण.
"Forts are the very soul of the kingdom."
— Adnyapatra (Ramchandra Pant Amatya)
Historical Authenticity ऐतिहासिक सत्यता
Bakhars
Historical chronicles written in Marathi during and after his reign, providing profound insights into the administrative and military setup.
Original Letters
Direct correspondence bearing the Royal Seal (Rajmudra) verifying his decrees for civilian protection and religious tolerance.
Foreign Records
Accounts from British, Portuguese, and Dutch merchants corroborate his genius in naval warfare and formidable presence.

FOUNDER OF HINDAVI SWARAJYA · 1630 – 1680

"Praised by Friends and Respected by Foes — the standard of a truly great King."

"महाराजांनी केवळ राज्य केले नाही, तर त्यांनी एका राष्ट्राची अस्मिता जागवली, रयतेला सन्मानाने आणि ताठ मानेने जगण्याची शिकवण दिली."
